# KushLife as the flagship (marketing)

## Gaps to fix first
- **"Weekly" isn't true for every plan.** Essential gets 1 visit a month and Care Plus gets 2 (`plans.ts`).
- **The WhatsApp digest goes to the elder's own phones, not the family abroad** (`family_digest_sender.py`). Family users have no phone number stored.

## Promise and taglines
**Promise:** "Every visit, we check how your parent is really living, and tell you that day."

**Taglines:**
- "Know they're okay. Every visit."
- "Not just a visit. A KushLife check."
- "See the trend, not just the emergency."

**Story for an NRI daughter:**
- **Fear:** "Amma says she's fine. I hear about the fall weeks later."
- **What changes:** 4 checks and a note on every visit.
- **Reassurance:** "4 green ticks on Monday in Dubai; they called me when Steady dipped."

**How it compares:**
- An ordinary caregiver visit says only "done". KushLife is the same checks every time, plus a trend.
- CCTV gives no dignity. KushLife is a human judgement with no camera.
- A relative's call is irregular and biased.
- Competitors such as Anvayaa, Emoha, Samarth and KITES (to verify) don't brand a scored, repeatable per-visit check. Own that word.

**One story:** Assess (Independence Score™) → Fix → Watch (KushLife) → Respond (SOS, BLS/CPR team).
"The home gets a score once. Your parent gets a KushLife check every visit."

## What customers get
**Weekly WhatsApp/email card** (Monday 08:00 in the family's own timezone):
- score
- 4 lines, one per check, ✅ or 🟡, with a short note
- next visit time in IST and local time
- a link
- "Reply 1 to request a call"

**Monthly KushLife letter (PDF):**
- score for the month
- the 4-check grid
- care manager's note
- visit log and photos
- actions taken
- Independence Score status
- next month's plan
- invoice

**Streaks:** "12 weeks steady", "50 checks". Never turn a decline into a game.

**Siblings:** invite them into the circle; each picks a channel and timezone; the cost can be split.

## Pricing
- KushLife in every plan, first line on each plan card: "1 / 2 / 4 / 4+ a month".
- Card frequency follows the plan.
- **NRI Complete:** a monthly video review.
- **Pay-as-you-go:** a check on each visit.
- **Trial:** first 4 KushLife visits at an intro price.
- **Referral:** "Gift a KushLife month".
