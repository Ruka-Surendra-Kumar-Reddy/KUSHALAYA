# Recitals

A. KushAlaya offers home-safety, family support and elder care services to senior citizens and their families in Hyderabad.

B. KushAlaya wishes to engage the Vendor, as an independent contractor, to carry out part of those services using the Vendor's own trained personnel. The Vendor states that it is legally qualified, licensed, insured and able to do so, and KushAlaya relies on that statement.

# 1. Definitions

1.1 “Applicable Law” means all laws, rules, regulations and orders in force in India and Telangana, including the Labour Codes (wages, industrial relations, social security, and occupational safety, health and working conditions) and rules under them, the Contract Act, 1872, GST and income tax laws, the MSMED Act, 2006, the Sexual Harassment of Women at Workplace Act, 2013, the Information Technology Act, 2000 and the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 with its Rules.

1.2 “Claim” means any claim, demand, complaint, action, investigation or proceeding by a Customer, Personnel, a regulator or any other person.

1.3 “Client Data” means all information about KushAlaya's Customers, their families, homes, health, schedules, access arrangements and payments, and all KushAlaya business information, in any form.

1.4 “Customer” means a person or family receiving KushAlaya's services, their family members and their property.

1.5 “Loss” means all losses, liabilities, damages, penalties, interest, costs and expenses, including reasonable legal and investigation fees and any refund, repair or compensation KushAlaya pays to a Customer.

1.6 “Personnel” means every individual deployed by the Vendor to perform the Services, including replacements and subcontractors' staff.

1.7 “Services” means the services described in each Service Order (Annexure A).

1.8 “Statutory Dues” means wages, overtime, bonus, gratuity, leave encashment, PF, ESI, retrenchment or other compensation, insurance premiums, professional tax, TDS on wages and any other amount payable to or for Personnel under Applicable Law or their terms of engagement.

1.9 “Vendor Default” means any act, omission, error, negligence, wilful misconduct, fraud, breach of this Agreement or Applicable Law, or incorrect, defective or unsafe work, advice or conduct, by the Vendor, its Personnel, its subcontractors or anyone for whom it is responsible. It applies whether or not KushAlaya gave general instructions, inspected, approved or accepted the work.

# 2. Appointment, scope and term

2.1 The Vendor shall perform the Services described in Service Orders signed by both Parties. Each Service Order forms part of this Agreement. If they conflict, this Agreement prevails unless the Service Order expressly says otherwise.

2.2 Limits of the Services. Unless a Service Order names the role and the Vendor holds the required skills and licences, the Vendor shall not, and shall ensure Personnel do not: (a) give medical advice or diagnoses; (b) perform nursing or clinical procedures; (c) administer or change medicines, beyond reminding a Customer to take medicine already prescribed; (d) handle Customers' money, valuables, documents or bank instruments; or (e) carry out electrical, plumbing, structural or other technical work.

2.3 This Agreement starts on {start_date} and continues for 12 months unless ended earlier under Clause 18. It may be renewed in writing.

2.4 KushAlaya has no obligation to engage a minimum number of Personnel or to give exclusivity, unless a Service Order says so.

# 3. Independent contractor and sole responsibility

3.1 The Vendor is an independent contractor. Personnel are employees or engaged workers of the Vendor only. No employment, agency, partnership or joint venture exists between KushAlaya and any Personnel or the Vendor.

3.2 The Vendor alone recruits, appoints, pays, trains, supervises, directs the manner of work of, disciplines and terminates Personnel. KushAlaya's instructions are limited to timing, location, safety rules and service standards.

3.3 The Vendor has no authority to bind KushAlaya, to make any promise, warranty or representation to a Customer on KushAlaya's behalf, or to collect payment from a Customer.

3.4 Identity cards and uniforms shall show the Vendor's name. They may refer to KushAlaya only as “service partner of KushAlaya”.

3.5 Sole responsibility. As between the Parties, the Vendor is solely responsible for every Vendor Default, and for the safety, insurance, injury compensation and Statutory Dues of its Personnel. KushAlaya has no liability to the Vendor, Personnel, Customers or any other person for a Vendor Default.

3.6 If any Personnel is held to be KushAlaya's employee, the Vendor shall indemnify KushAlaya under Clause 10.

# 4. Personnel: verification, deployment and replacement

4.1 Verification before deployment. Before deploying any individual, the Vendor shall complete and give KushAlaya proof of: (a) identity and address verification, with lawful handling of that data; (b) police verification or clearance certificate not older than 6 months; (c) previous employer or reference check; (d) medical fitness certificate, and vaccination status where KushAlaya requires it for work with seniors; (e) proof of claimed qualifications or training; and (f) proof the individual is at least 18 years old.

4.2 Approval. The Vendor shall submit the form at Annexure C for each individual. KushAlaya may reject any individual without giving reasons. Approval by KushAlaya does not reduce the Vendor's responsibility for that individual.

4.3 Replacement. The Vendor shall replace any Personnel within 24 hours (emergency) or 3 working days (other cases) of KushAlaya's written request on grounds of performance, conduct, safety, Customer complaint or absence, at no extra cost.

4.4 Continuity. The Vendor shall keep a backup pool of at least [__]% of deployed strength.

4.5 Standards. Personnel shall be trained, shall treat senior citizens with dignity and patience, and shall follow KushAlaya's service protocols and the Code of Conduct (Annexure D).

4.6 No minors or unapproved persons. The Vendor shall not deploy minors or send anyone not approved under Clause 4.2 to a Customer's home.

4.7 Re-verification. The Vendor shall re-verify Personnel annually and immediately after any complaint or incident.

# 5. Standard of performance

5.1 The Vendor shall perform the Services with reasonable skill, care and diligence, using trained and supervised Personnel, and in line with Applicable Law and the Service Order.

5.2 The Vendor shall keep a named supervisor reachable during service hours and shall respond to KushAlaya's calls within 120 minutes.

5.3 The Vendor shall acknowledge any Customer complaint within 24 hours, tell KushAlaya, and fix the cause at its own cost.

5.4 No acceptance defence. KushAlaya's inspection, checks, approval, sign-off, payment or use of any work or Personnel does not reduce the Vendor's responsibility and does not make KushAlaya responsible for any defect, error or Vendor Default.

5.5 The Vendor shall redo defective work promptly at its own cost. Service levels and penalties are in the Service Order.

6.2 The Vendor shall comply with Applicable Law for Personnel, including minimum wages, hours, overtime, rest, leave, bonus, gratuity, social security, safety, welfare, and the ban on child and forced labour.

6.3 KushAlaya provides only the facilities that Applicable Law requires of a principal employer. The Vendor provides all others.

6.4 The Vendor shall tell KushAlaya within 30 working days of any notice, inspection, penalty, prosecution, suspension or cancellation involving the Vendor or Personnel.

6.5 If the Vendor's licence lapses or is cancelled, KushAlaya may suspend the Services and payment immediately and terminate under Clause 18.2.

7.3 KushAlaya need not pay an invoice until the Clause 7.2 documents are received and found satisfactory.

7.4 The Vendor shall give each worker a pay slip, keep all records the law requires, settle all dues on exit and obtain no-dues acknowledgments for Personnel leaving the engagement.

8.1 KushAlaya shall pay the fees in the Service Order, which include all Vendor costs and Statutory Dues, plus GST shown separately.

8.5 If the Vendor's GST default makes KushAlaya lose input tax credit, KushAlaya may recover the credit lost, with interest and penalty, from the Vendor and set it off against payments.

8.7 No fee increase applies unless agreed in writing.

The Vendor shall not contact or settle with a Customer on a matter involving KushAlaya without KushAlaya's written consent.

9.3 Authorities. KushAlaya may inform police, regulators and insurers of any incident. The Vendor shall cooperate fully.

9.4 No contribution. The Vendor has no right of contribution, reimbursement or set-off against KushAlaya for a Vendor Default, even where a Claim is made against both Parties.

9.6 KushAlaya's liability. KushAlaya's total liability to the Vendor under or in connection with this Agreement is limited to fees properly due and unpaid for Services performed, and shall not exceed the fees paid in the previous 3 months.

9.7 Neither Party is liable to the other for indirect or consequential loss or loss of profit, except under the indemnity in Clause 10. Nothing in this Clause excludes liability that cannot be excluded by law, or a Party's own fraud or wilful misconduct.

10.1 The Vendor shall indemnify, defend and hold harmless KushAlaya, its directors, officers and employees (the “Indemnified Parties”) against all Loss arising from or relating to: (a) any Vendor Default; (b) non-payment or late payment of Statutory Dues, or any claim by Personnel for wages, benefits, reinstatement, regularisation or being treated as KushAlaya's employees; (c) any breach of Applicable Law by the Vendor or Personnel, including any amount KushAlaya must pay as principal employer; (d) death, injury, illness or property damage caused by Personnel or the Vendor's negligence; (e) theft, fraud, breach of trust, assault, harassment, abuse or any criminal act by Personnel; (f) incorrect, defective or unsafe work, products, installations or advice; (g) breach of Clause 14, including regulatory penalties and notification costs; (h) infringement of third-party rights by materials the Vendor supplies; (i) tax or GST default by the Vendor; and (j) any false statement in the Vendor's warranties or certificates.

10.2 Claims procedure. KushAlaya shall tell the Vendor of any Claim within a reasonable time. KushAlaya may control the defence and settlement of any Claim made by a Customer, regulator or the media, because it affects its brand, and shall consult the Vendor in good faith. For other Claims, the Vendor may defend with counsel reasonably acceptable to KushAlaya but shall not settle any Claim that imposes an obligation or admission on KushAlaya without its consent. Late notice reduces the Vendor's liability only to the extent the Vendor is actually prejudiced.

10.3 The Vendor shall pay indemnified amounts within 15 days of written demand with supporting records.

10.4 This indemnity survives termination.

# 13. Safety, conduct and incident reporting

13.1 Personnel shall follow KushAlaya's service protocols and the Code of Conduct (Annexure D) at all times, particularly in Customers' homes.

13.2 The Vendor shall ensure Personnel do not: (a) accept cash, gifts, loans or keys from Customers beyond what KushAlaya authorises; (b) handle Customers' valuables, documents, medicines or bank instruments; (c) work under the influence of alcohol or drugs; (d) harass, abuse or discriminate against anyone; or (e) take photographs or recordings inside homes unless authorised.

13.3 The Vendor shall give Personnel induction and refresher training on senior care, safe handling, hygiene, emergency response and privacy, and keep training records.

13.4 Incident reporting. The Vendor shall report to KushAlaya within 2 hours any accident, injury, theft, abuse allegation, medical emergency, Customer complaint of misconduct or police involvement, shall preserve all related records, messages and footage, and shall cooperate fully with KushAlaya and the authorities.

13.5 KushAlaya may remove any Personnel from a site immediately on safety or conduct grounds, pending investigation.

# 14. Confidentiality and data protection

14.1 The Vendor and Personnel shall keep Client Data and KushAlaya's business information confidential, use it only to perform the Services, and disclose it only to Personnel who need it and are bound by written confidentiality duties (Annexure D). 14.3 The Vendor is responsible for lawful handling of Personnel's own data, including ID and police verification records.

14.4 The Vendor shall ensure Personnel do not copy Customer data, share house access details or post about Customers on social media.

14.5 Breach of this Clause allows immediate termination and injunctive relief, in addition to other remedies.

18.4 Handover. On expiry or termination, the Vendor shall for up to 30 days continue the Services if asked, hand over all KushAlaya materials, keys, devices, branded items and records, and return or delete Client Data.
